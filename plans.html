Plans & programmes

How Kerala sets its development priorities, funds them each year, and checks that they deliver.

Fourteenth Five-Year Plan (2022-27)

The Fourteenth Plan aims to build a knowledge economy, modernise Kerala’s production sectors (agriculture, industry and services) and protect the social gains the state is known for. It was prepared with sector working groups of experts, officials and civil society.

  • Higher productivity and incomes in agriculture and allied sectors.
  • Growth of industry, start-ups and the knowledge economy, creating skilled jobs.
  • Climate-resilient infrastructure, flood and landslide preparedness.
  • Better quality in public health and education.
  • Eradicating extreme poverty and supporting vulnerable groups.

Annual Plans

Each year the Board works with every department to turn the Five-Year Plan into a scheme-wise Annual Plan. Outlays are presented with the State Budget, and a fixed share flows to local governments for their own plans.

Economic Review

The Economic Review is the official yearly survey of Kerala’s economy. Economic Review 2025, released in January 2026 ahead of the State Budget, reports that Kerala’s GSDP grew 6.19 per cent in real terms in 2024-25, with services and IT leading the growth.

It is published in English and Malayalam, with a statistical appendix.

Decentralised planning

Since the People’s Plan Campaign of 1996, panchayats and municipalities have prepared their own development plans. Residents set priorities in grama sabhas and ward sabhas; working groups draft projects; and District Planning Committees vet and approve them.

The Board issues the guidelines for this process, trains local planners and reviews how well local plans connect with state priorities.

Plan monitoring

The Board tracks the expenditure and physical progress of plan schemes through the year and reports to government on bottlenecks, so funds can be redirected where they will do the most good.

Evaluation

The Evaluation Division conducts field studies of selected schemes. Findings are published and fed into the next Annual Plan.
