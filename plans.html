Fourteenth Five-Year Plan (2022-27)

The Fourteenth Plan began in 2022-23. It was prepared with 44 working groups involving more than 1,200 experts, officials and representatives of civil society. 2026-27 is the final year of the Plan.

Fifteenth Five-Year Plan (2027-32)

The Board has begun preparing the Fifteenth Five-Year Plan. It has decided to empanel consulting firms and think tanks for technical support, will constitute sectoral working groups, and is inviting citizens’ views through Kerala Plan.

Annual Plans

Each year the Board works with every department to turn the Five-Year Plan into a scheme-wise Annual Plan. The Annual Plan is presented with the State Budget, and a fixed share of plan funds flows to local governments for their own projects.

Decentralised Planning

Since the People’s Plan Campaign of 1996, panchayats, municipalities and corporations prepare their own development plans. Residents set priorities in grama sabhas and ward sabhas, working groups prepare projects, and District Planning Committees vet and approve them. The Board issues the guidelines, trains local planners and reviews local plans.

Monitoring & Evaluation

The Board tracks the expenditure and physical progress of plan schemes through the year and reports to government on bottlenecks. The Evaluation Division conducts independent field studies of selected schemes; findings are published and fed into the next Annual Plan.
