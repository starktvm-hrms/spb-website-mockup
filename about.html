About the Board

The Kerala State Planning Board is the apex body for development planning in Kerala. Constituted in 1967, it functions under the Planning and Economic Affairs Department of the Government of Kerala, with its office at Pattom, Thiruvananthapuram.

Kerala is one of the few states that continued to prepare Five-Year Plans alongside Annual Plans after national planning changed in 2015. The Board prepares both, and links state priorities with the plans of more than a thousand local governments.

Functions

  • Formulate the Five-Year Plans and Annual Plans of the state.
  • Assess the state’s financial, material and human resources and recommend how to use them.
  • Monitor the implementation of plan schemes by departments.
  • Evaluate schemes and programmes, and recommend corrective measures.
  • Guide and support plan formulation by local governments.
  • Prepare the annual Economic Review placed before the Legislative Assembly.
  • Advise the government on development policy questions referred to the Board.

Board Members

The Chief Minister is the Chairperson of the Board. The Board includes a Vice Chairperson, ministers nominated as members, expert members and a Member Secretary. It was last reconstituted in July 2026.

History

  • 1967 — State Planning Board constituted.
  • 1996 — People’s Plan Campaign: a large share of plan funds devolved to local governments, planned through grama sabhas.
  • 2022 — Fourteenth Five-Year Plan (2022-27) launched with 44 working groups.
  • 2026 — Board reconstituted; preparation of the Fifteenth Five-Year Plan (2027-32) begins.
