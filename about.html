About the State Planning Board

Kerala’s apex body for development planning since 1967: preparing the state’s plans, advising government on the economy and supporting planning by local governments.

Our mandate

The Kerala State Planning Board advises the Government of Kerala on development policy and prepares the state’s Five-Year and Annual Plans. It assesses the resources available to the state, sets priorities across sectors and recommends how plan funds should be allocated.

Once plans are approved, the Board monitors how departments and local governments carry them out, and commissions evaluation studies to learn what works. Each year it publishes the Economic Review, the official account of Kerala’s economy, placed before the Legislative Assembly with the State Budget.

Functions

  • Formulate Five-Year Plans and Annual Plans for the state.
  • Assess the state’s material, capital and human resources and how to use them.
  • Monitor and review plan implementation by departments.
  • Evaluate schemes and programmes, and recommend changes.
  • Guide and support plan formulation by panchayats and municipalities.
  • Prepare the annual Economic Review and other studies on Kerala’s economy.
  • Advise government on policy questions referred to the Board.

Structure of the Board

The Chief Minister of Kerala is the Chairperson of the Board. A Vice Chairperson leads its day-to-day work, supported by ministers nominated as members, full-time expert members and a Member Secretary who heads the secretariat.

The Board was reconstituted in July 2026. Names of the Vice Chairperson, members and Member Secretary will be listed here from the official notification.

Divisions

Each division is led by a Chief and staffed by economists, statisticians and sector specialists.

Plan Coordination

Assembles the Five-Year and Annual Plans, resource estimates and the Economic Review.

Perspective Planning

Long-term studies, macro-economic modelling and future-facing policy work.

Agriculture

Agriculture, allied sectors, irrigation, fisheries, food security and rural development.

Industry & Infrastructure

Industry, MSMEs, IT, power, transport, roads and urban infrastructure.

Social Services

Education, health, housing, social security, gender and welfare of marginalised groups.

Decentralised Planning

Guidelines, training and support for local government plans and District Planning Committees.

Evaluation

Independent evaluation studies of state and centrally sponsored schemes.

Plan Monitoring & IT

Plan expenditure tracking, data systems and the Board’s digital services.

History

Six decades of planning

  1. 1967State Planning Board constituted to prepare Kerala’s development plans.
  2. 1996People’s Plan Campaign: about a third of plan funds devolved to local governments, planned through grama sabhas.
  3. 2017-22Thirteenth Five-Year Plan, with flagship missions for housing, health, education and the environment.
  4. 2022-27Fourteenth Five-Year Plan focused on a knowledge economy and modern production base.
  5. 2026Board reconstituted; Kerala Plan citizen forum launched.
Kerala model

Development that reaches the ward

Kerala’s planning is known for pairing high human development with deep democratic decentralisation. Every panchayat and municipality plans its own projects with residents, and the Board makes sure state priorities and local needs meet.
