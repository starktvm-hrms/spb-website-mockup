About the Board

Planning for Kerala’s development

The Kerala State Planning Board is the state’s apex body for development planning, constituted in 1967 under the Planning and Economic Affairs Department.

The Board formulates the Five-Year and Annual Plans, assesses the state’s resources, monitors plan implementation and evaluates schemes. Every year it prepares the Economic Review, placed before the Legislative Assembly with the State Budget.

Since the People’s Plan Campaign of 1996, the Board has also guided panchayats and municipalities as they prepare their own development plans with residents.
