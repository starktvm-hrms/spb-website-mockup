    About Plan Keralam

    The State Planning Board shapes the roads you travel, the schools your children attend, the water you drink and the hospitals you rely on. Tell it what daily life looks like where you live.

    Share a concern, an idea or an opinion on the things that shape daily life: your plate, your commute, your classroom, your hospital, your village. The State Planning Board reads it here.

    Pick a topic and start the first conversation. Name your panchayat or ward so the Board can act on it.

    How Plan Keralam works

    1. SharePost a concern, idea, opinion or question under the topic it belongs to. Name your district or ward.
    2. Like, comment and shareLike what matters, reply with what you know and share posts with your neighbours. The most backed posts rise to the top.
    3. Hear backBoard staff can reply in the thread. Their answers carry an Official badge.

    House rules

    • Be specific: name the place, ward or panchayat.
    • Criticise schemes and decisions, not people.
    • Do not share phone numbers or home addresses.
    • Replies marked Official come from the Board.
